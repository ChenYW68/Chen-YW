Social determinants of health are key drivers of cardiovascular disease, but their relationships differ across regions. This study proposes a high-dimensional county clustering approach with social-determinants-domain-aware regularization to identify regional heterogeneity in social determinants of health and cardiovascular disease associations in the United States.
